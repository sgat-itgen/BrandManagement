# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Brand Management to be a respectful, practical, and psychologically safe project for everyone who contributes code, documentation, design, testing, operations, or feedback.

We expect participants to communicate professionally, assume good intent while investigating disagreements, and focus criticism on the work rather than on people.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, inclusive, and constructive.
- Explain technical concerns with evidence and actionable alternatives.
- Welcome different levels of experience, backgrounds, and perspectives.
- Give credit for ideas, reports, fixes, and review work.
- Keep sensitive business, security, and personal information private.
- Accept responsible correction and revise decisions when new evidence appears.
- Follow the repository’s contribution, security, and review practices.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention, unwanted contact, or unwelcome advances.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, bad-faith review, or repeated derailing.
- Publishing private information, credentials, session data, or business documents without authorization.
- Retaliating against someone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.
- Any conduct that would violate applicable law or company policy.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, chats, meetings, demos, deployment discussions, and any other forum used for project work. It applies when someone is acting as a project participant, regardless of role or seniority.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the approved internal company channel or the repository owner. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports.

Reports should include what happened, when and where it happened, people involved, relevant links or evidence, and any immediate safety or confidentiality concern. Do not forward private documents or credentials unnecessarily.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality as far as practical, avoid retaliation, and take action proportionate to the situation. Possible actions include clarification, a private warning, moderation of project access, temporary or permanent removal from project spaces, or escalation under company policy.

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, restrict participation, or escalate behavior that conflicts with this code even when it does not fit a single example above.

## Good-faith participation

Reports made in good faith are protected from retaliation. Deliberately false reports, doxxing, threats, or attempts to misuse the reporting process are themselves unacceptable behavior.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant. The project may apply additional company policies and local procedures where they provide stronger protections or more specific guidance.
